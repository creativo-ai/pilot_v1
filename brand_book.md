# Creativo Brand Book

## 1. Brand Identity

### Brand Name
Creativo

### Tagline
*"Where Strategy Meets Creativity"*

### Brand Story
Creativo was founded with one mission: to help small and medium-sized businesses tell their story in a way that resonates, converts, and inspires. We believe every brand has a unique voice — our job is to find it, sharpen it, and amplify it.

---

## 2. Mission, Vision & Values

### Mission
Empower brands to tell their story creatively and effectively.

### Vision
To be the leading creative agency recognized for impactful branding campaigns.

### Core Values
- **Creativity** — We push beyond the obvious to find ideas that surprise and delight.
- **Collaboration** — We work as partners, not vendors.
- **Excellence** — We hold ourselves to the highest standards in every deliverable.
- **Customer Focus** — Our clients' success is our success.

---

## 3. Target Audience

### Primary Audience
Small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) looking to establish or grow their brand presence.

### Audience Characteristics
- Business owners and marketing managers
- Companies with 10–500 employees
- Industries: retail, e-commerce, hospitality, professional services
- Pain points: lack of consistent brand voice, low engagement, unclear messaging

### Audience Communication Style
- Speak to them as partners, not customers
- Use clear, jargon-free language
- Focus on outcomes and ROI, not just creativity

---

## 4. Brand Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice
Strategic yet approachable. We are experts who don't talk down to our clients.

### Tone Guidelines
- **Professional** — We are credible and reliable.
- **Creative** — We bring fresh ideas and unexpected angles.
- **Friendly** — We are warm, human, and easy to work with.
- **Confident** — We back our recommendations with strategy and data.

### Tone by Context
| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Email campaigns | Warm, persuasive, benefit-driven |
| Social media | Playful, engaging, conversational |
| Proposals | Confident, strategic, professional |
| Error messages | Empathetic, helpful, solution-focused |

---

## 5. Messaging Framework

### Core Message
Creativo transforms brands through creative strategy and storytelling.

### Key Messages
1. We deliver campaigns that drive real business results.
2. We combine creative thinking with data-driven strategy.
3. We treat every client as a long-term partner.
4. We make the complex simple and the simple compelling.

### Words We Use
- Empower, transform, elevate, craft, amplify, strategic, impactful, authentic

### Words We Avoid
- Cheap, basic, simple (as a negative), just, merely, obviously

## 6. Visual Identity

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Deep Purple (#5B2D8E) — represents creativity and sophistication
- **Secondary:** Vibrant Orange (#FF6B35) — represents energy and action
- **Neutral:** Charcoal (#2D2D2D) — for body text and backgrounds
- **Light:** Soft White (#F9F9F9) — for backgrounds and breathing space

### Typography
- **Headings:** Playfair Display (serif) — elegant and authoritative
- **Body:** Inter (sans-serif) — clean and highly readable
- **Accent:** Montserrat Bold — for CTAs and highlights

### Logo Usage
- Always maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "C" in Creativo
- Never stretch, rotate, or recolor the logo
- Use the white version on dark backgrounds only

---

## 7. Content Guidelines

### Social Media
- Instagram: Visual-first, aspirational, behind-the-scenes content
- LinkedIn: Thought leadership, case studies, industry insights
- Facebook: Community engagement, campaign announcements, promotions
- TikTok: Creative process, quick tips, brand personality

### Email Marketing
- Subject lines: 40–60 characters, benefit-driven, personalized when possible
- Opening: Always address the reader's pain point or goal
- CTA: One clear action per email
- Sign-off: "Best regards, Creativo Team"

### Caption Writing
- Start with a hook (question, bold statement, or surprising fact)
- Keep Instagram captions under 150 words
- Always include a CTA
- Use 3–5 relevant hashtags maximum

---

## 8. Do's and Don'ts

### Do's
- Always lead with the client's benefit
- Use active voice
- Be specific — use numbers and outcomes when possible
- Match the platform's native style

### Don'ts
- Don't use generic stock phrases ("synergy", "game-changer", "revolutionary")
- Don't make promises we can't keep
- Don't ignore brand tone guidelines for the sake of trends
- Don't post without proofreading

---

## 9. Campaign Strategy Principles

### Campaign Framework
1. **Discovery** — Understand the brand, audience, and goals
2. **Strategy** — Define the message, channel mix, and KPIs
3. **Creation** — Produce content aligned with brand guidelines
4. **Launch** — Execute across channels with consistent messaging
5. **Optimize** — Measure results and refine

### Success Metrics
- Engagement rate (target: >3% on social)
- Email open rate (target: >25%)
- Conversion rate (target: varies by campaign)
- Brand recall (measured via surveys)

---

## 10. Customer Service Voice

### Principles
- Respond within 24 hours
- Always acknowledge the customer's concern first
- Offer a solution, not just an apology
- End every interaction on a positive note

### Sample Phrases
- "We hear you, and here's how we'll make it right..."
- "Great question — here's what we recommend..."
- "We appreciate your patience while we look into this..."
